# Market Maker / Marvel FX Method: notes and testable rules

Our own summary of John's course material. **Not course text**: everything is
paraphrased, with a source after each point. First written 2026-11-10 from the
PDF, extended the same day from all 21 video transcripts.

Sources:

- **PDF**: `Market-Maker-Method-SquirrelFX.pdf`, module 1 (27 pages, Spanish), cited as `p.N`.
- **Videos** (Spanish, transcripts in `~/jvd-outputs/trading/transcripts/`, `[mm:ss]` lines), cited as `(name @ mm:ss)`:
  - "Lo demas" folder: `Niveles MFXM`, `PEAK FORMATIONS 1`, `Marcar Entradas Y Niveles`, `Exponential Moving Average`, `Price Action Parte 1`, `Price Action 2`, `Mejores Entradas despues de noticias` (shortened to `Noticias`), `sin titulo` (a daily webinar).
  - "teoria" folder: `Teoria 1` to `Teoria 5` (The Marvel FX Method Parte 1-5).
  - "practica" folder: `Practica 1` (file "Parte practica 1") to `Practica 8`. These are reviews of real trades.
- Speakers differ: `Niveles MFXM` is presented by one teacher, the teoria and most practica videos by another, and some practica videos are given in Spain time. Where they disagree we say so.

A word on tone: the videos often call setups "guaranteed" (garantizada, "fijo"), "100%" or "never fails". We treat every such claim as something to test, not a fact (`PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 04:44`; `sin titulo @ 18:12`; `Teoria 2 @ 13:49`, `@ 16:44`; `Teoria 4 @ 39:24`; `Practica 8 @ 03:36`).

## The idea in one paragraph

Large dealers ("market makers") need retail traders on the wrong side. They
set a range, push price past it to trigger stops (the stop hunt), then move
the real way slowly. The trader's job is to recognize the trap, wait for it
to finish, and trade with the market maker instead of against it (p.2, p.7, p.25).
The teoria videos describe each day in three phases: range in Asia, stop hunt, then the real move the other way (`Teoria 4 @ 00:26`).
A calm, steady move is the real trend; a sudden violent move is the trap (`sin titulo @ 30:42`; `Teoria 2 @ 29:05`; `Practica 8 @ 01:06`).

## Levels (niveles): how they are drawn

Two meanings are used, and they conflict:

- **Intraday pushes** (`Niveles MFXM`): price moves in cycles of three pushes (impulsos). A push plus its pause is one level: level 1, 2, 3 (`Niveles MFXM @ 00:18`, `@ 15:37`). The teoria videos also count three intraday pushes of 20-40 pips each after the fake break, watched on 1h or 30m (`Teoria 2 @ 03:23`), and call them "vectors" in the practica videos (`Practica 2 @ 07:26`; `Practica 4 @ 15:45`).
- **Multi-day phases** (`Teoria 2`): a level is a rise or fall that runs about three days (`Teoria 2 @ 00:52`). Level 1 is fast and driven by market makers, level 2 slower and driven by retail, level 3 fast again, with stop hunts and panic (`Teoria 2 @ 01:02`, `@ 01:07`, `@ 01:19`). Level 1 can take a day and level 3's consolidation 2-3 days (`Teoria 4 @ 03:15`).

Common points:

- Up cycle: W, then V, V (pullbacks), then M. Down cycle: M, then A, A, then W (`Niveles MFXM @ 03:00`; `PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 07:56`; `Teoria 1 @ 12:25`; p.16, p.25).
- After the third push, expect a longer pullback, a failed try at a fourth, then a new cycle the other way. Do not trade the fourth push (`Niveles MFXM @ 01:00`, `@ 10:29`). Level 3 of one move becomes level 1 of the next (`Teoria 2 @ 02:34`). Sometimes there is a fourth level or the count runs 2.5 or 4 days (`Teoria 2 @ 02:48`).
- Telling the levels apart (`Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 01:13`):
  - Level 1: a small, short pause.
  - Level 2: a bigger pause without many wicks.
  - Level 3: a long consolidation that tests the same low (or high) many times; one example sat there about a day and a half (`PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 13:43`).
- EMAs help to tell the level: close together at levels 1 and 2, far apart and steep at level 3 (`Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 17:15`; `Teoria 4 @ 08:28`; `Practica 4 @ 23:57`).
- Level 3 pays the most (`Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 02:02`; `Teoria 2 @ 01:19`); a level 3 trade can make 150-200 pips in a couple of hours (`Teoria 3 @ 17:54`). Catching level 1 on day 1 can become a 3-day swing (`Teoria 2 @ 29:31`).
- Consolidations between levels have stop runs of 20-30 pips both ways and create fake support and resistance (`Teoria 2 @ 07:06`; p.25).
- Draw levels on the 1h chart, time the entry on 15m (`Niveles MFXM @ 02:55`, `@ 08:51`). The teoria speaker says levels matter less to him because he trades intraday (`Teoria 2 @ 05:50`; `Practica 4 @ 23:48`).
- Only trade clean charts (`Niveles MFXM @ 06:12`; `Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 14:47`; `Practica 2 @ 00:31`).
- Practice: mark levels and winning entries on old charts as "flashcards" (`Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 00:44`, `@ 06:44`).

## Peak formations (formación pico) and M/W

- A peak formation is the big M (or W) at the end of a level 3, often at a new high (or low) of the week that quickly returns inside (`PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 07:12`, `@ 22:40`; `sin titulo @ 13:33`).
- Intraday version: after Asia, price breaks to a high of day, pulls back, rallies again and either pokes above that high or fails to reach it, then drops. That is the M; the W is the mirror (`Teoria 1 @ 10:41`).
- **Always enter on the second leg (segunda pata)**, never the first. With no second leg there is no trade (`Teoria 3 @ 16:57`; `Teoria 5 @ 12:02`; `Practica 1 @ 19:37`, `@ 50:24`). The first leg needs a stop of 20-25 pips; the second leg about 10 (`Teoria 3 @ 21:17`; `Teoria 5 @ 07:14`; `Practica 2 @ 07:54`).
- The second leg should **not** make a new high (or low) of day; the trade is valid while that extreme holds (`Practica 1 @ 21:13`, `@ 54:47`). A slightly deeper second low is accepted if the TDI shows divergence (`Practica 1 @ 19:46`). The older videos instead describe the second extreme as the stop hunt past the first (`PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 20:22`, `@ 22:02`).
- Only four setups exist: stop hunt high (M), stop hunt low (W), straight fall (A), straight rise (V). Nothing else is traded (`Teoria 2 @ 19:53`, `@ 21:41`, `@ 21:52`; p.27).
- Flags, triangles and head and shoulders are just M/W variants and traps; breakout trading, Fibonacci and AB=CD are called myths (`Teoria 4 @ 12:07`, `@ 12:40`; `Teoria 3 @ 11:41`). This contradicts the price-action videos, which use Fibonacci (see below).
- Timing: an M or W should form in 30-120 minutes; one built over many hours is weak (`Practica 2 @ 12:12`; `Teoria 3 @ 14:13`). Market makers may hold price at the extreme up to 2 hours before turning (`Teoria 3 @ 08:12`).
- A triple top or bottom at the extreme counts (`Practica 6 @ 04:43`; `Practica 2 @ 15:30`; `Practica 5 @ 15:48`).
- The fake move out of Asia is often three fast "vector" candles of similar size, up to about 50 pips (`Teoria 1 @ 22:45`; `Teoria 2 @ 33:57`). One 15m candle of 20-30 pips can be the whole stop hunt (`Teoria 1 @ 29:19`).
- A wick past a level does not count as a break if the candle closes back inside (`Practica 1 @ 58:14`).
- Once price leaves a peak formation, only look for trades in the new direction for about three days (`PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 09:56`, `@ 13:24`). After a big M comes an A, another A, then a W (`PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 16:44`).
- If anything is unclear, don't enter (`Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 04:56`; `sin titulo @ 32:09`; `Practica 1 @ 53:25`).

## EMA use

Two EMA sets are taught:

- **12 / 36 / 200 / 633** on Heikin-Ashi candles, a swing method (`Exponential Moving Average @ 02:08`, `@ 03:00`).
  - The 12 hugs the trend; a break of the 12 usually tests the 36 (`@ 01:37`, `@ 04:00`).
  - Entry when the 12 and 36 come together and cross, ideally with price crossing several EMAs (`@ 10:43`, `@ 20:34`); confirmation grows as candles close past the 12, 36, 200 and 633 (`@ 11:25`).
  - A move through the 36 is the exit (`@ 11:02`). No entry when the 12 and 36 stay far apart (`@ 20:28`). Fails in ranges (`@ 12:00`).
  - Analysis goes month, week, day, 4h, then 1h for the entry (`@ 27:19`).
- **5 / 13 / 50 / 200 / 800** on 15m candles, the teoria and practica template (`Teoria 4 @ 04:05`; `Practica 1 @ 12:13`).
  - EMA 5 crossing 13 confirms the direction, but the cross is often late and is not the entry by itself (`Teoria 4 @ 05:01`; `Practica 2 @ 04:47`; `Practica 4 @ 28:18`). Use it only in a trend, not a range (`Practica 4 @ 28:54`).
  - The 50, 200 and 800 are mainly profit targets and support or resistance; break one and expect the next (`Practica 1 @ 07:05`; `Practica 4 @ 27:38`; `Teoria 4 @ 04:48`).
  - The 50 is "home": price comes back to it (`Teoria 4 @ 05:37`; `Noticias @ 25:29`). PDF: first target the 50, second the 200 (p.8).
  - Timeframe mapping: the 800 on 15m sits about where the 200 is on 1h and the 50 on 4h (`Practica 5 @ 00:55`, `@ 02:29`; `Teoria 4 @ 09:51`). The speaker got this backwards once and corrected it (`Practica 4 @ 25:09`).
  - A pin that barely breaks the 800 and closes back is an entry (`Practica 8 @ 02:42`, `@ 02:56`; `Practica 6 @ 06:44`).
- The `Noticias` video uses only 50 and 200 because the platform allowed two (`Noticias @ 24:24`). An 800 also shows up in `Niveles MFXM @ 13:58`.

## Entries after news (noticias)

- UK news at 4:30 AM New York time: Asia consolidates, a fake move, the news pushes hard one way, then at the New York open price reverses toward the day's open (`Noticias @ 00:27`, `@ 03:40`, `@ 15:11`). Preferred pairs: GBP crosses (`Noticias @ 16:05`).
- Confirm with the TDI leaving the band, coming back and crossing the signal line; prefer the second leg (`Noticias @ 08:22`, `@ 10:56`). Target back to the Asia range, about 40-50 pips (`Noticias @ 24:08`).
- The teoria videos say: don't trade the news itself (`Practica 1 @ 48:32`). A fast move right after news completes a setup and then reverses (`Teoria 3 @ 24:17`). On strong news pivots get blown through; use candle patterns only and wait for confirmation such as wicks at yesterday's low (`Teoria 4 @ 20:48`, `@ 21:39`).
- Example: a news spike broke resistance by 85-90 pips, was back inside in 15 minutes and 200 pips lower an hour later (`Practica 1 @ 47:25`).
- Around FOMC he moved trades to break-even or closed at zero instead of holding (`Practica 7 @ 00:42`, `@ 08:03`).
- News covers the planned move; the spike is the market maker (p.12, p.19).

## Price action

- Candles only count near the high or low of the day, not mid-range (`Teoria 3 @ 00:22`; `Teoria 2 @ 31:27`).
- Candles named as confirmation:
  - Hammer and inverted hammer (`Teoria 2 @ 31:32`; `Teoria 3 @ 04:10`).
  - Doji, which holds the extreme while orders fill (`Teoria 3 @ 02:45`; `Teoria 2 @ 32:36`).
  - Morning and evening star (`Teoria 3 @ 03:19`; `Teoria 2 @ 10:51`).
  - Spinning top (`Teoria 2 @ 31:48`).
  - Engulfing (`Practica 1 @ 51:17`).
  - "Railroad tracks" (vías del tren): two side-by-side candles of similar size and opposite color at the extreme (`Teoria 2 @ 32:52`; `Practica 3 @ 01:57`; `PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 01:49`).
  - Clusters of pin bars and long wicks that fail to break the extreme (`Practica 2 @ 01:06`; `Practica 1 @ 44:00`).
- Never enter on a forming candle; buy only after a green close, sell only after a red close (`Practica 1 @ 23:32`; `Teoria 5 @ 01:42`; `Teoria 3 @ 18:08`). Entry is the open of the next candle (`Practica 1 @ 41:20`).
- Enter in the consolidation, never during the fast move (`Teoria 3 @ 17:25`, `@ 24:48`).
- Price-action videos (another style): weekly support and resistance by counting touches, then daily zones, 4h trend lines and 1h channels (`Price Action Parte 1 @ 00:22`, `@ 03:47`); levels are zones (`@ 02:19`); Fibonacci 38.2-161.8 for confluence (`@ 09:10`; `Price Action 2 @ 00:27`); trend-line break, retest, entry (`Price Action 2 @ 07:24`).
- Divergence between price and the TDI/RSI is an extra confirmation (`PEAK FORMATIONS 1 @ 07:05`; `Teoria 5 @ 04:49`; `Practica 6 @ 00:28`).

## Sessions and time of day (New York time)

- The MetaTrader day starts at 5 PM (`Teoria 1 @ 02:51`; `sin titulo @ 02:07`; p.13). Spreads widen around 5 PM (`Teoria 1 @ 03:39`).
- 5-8 PM is a dead zone where the Asia range forms; don't trade it (`Teoria 1 @ 05:40`). The teoria speaker no longer trades Asia (`Teoria 4 @ 27:00`), though another video still notes Asia setups (`Teoria 3 @ 05:42`).
- His session labels: Asia about 8:30 PM-3 AM, London 3:30-9:30 AM, New York 9:30 AM-5 PM (`Teoria 1 @ 06:10`, `@ 06:22`, `@ 08:09`). These are his labels, not the standard opens.
- The two key 15m candles: 3:30-3:45 AM (London) and 9:30-9:45 AM (New York) (`Teoria 1 @ 10:18`; `Teoria 2 @ 04:01`). Entries around 3:45-4 AM and 9:45-10 AM (`Teoria 4 @ 29:43`; `Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 21:51`; `Practica 2 @ 02:39`). PDF: the 15 minutes after about 2:30 and 8:30 (p.13, p.23).
- Daily rhythm: Asia breakout around 1 AM, second leg of the M/W around 3-4 AM, faster moves from 8 AM with the New York second leg up to 11 AM (`Teoria 3 @ 26:02`, `@ 26:09`, `@ 26:16`). A New York reversal comes "almost always" between 9 and 11 (`Teoria 4 @ 33:13`).
- Trade only end of Asia into London, or New York, 2-4 hours at most; if nothing by 1 PM, stop for the day (`Teoria 2 @ 26:44`). He scans 9-11 AM (`Practica 7 @ 19:11`) and closes by noon (`Practica 8 @ 07:21`). New York is tradable until about 11:00-11:30 (`Marcar Entradas Y Niveles @ 22:24`).
- If London already paid, skip New York (`Teoria 4 @ 40:16`). Best days Tuesday to Thursday (`Practica 3 @ 13:09`). Holidays mean smaller moves (`Practica 4 @ 01:27`).
- The stop-hunt zone is 25-50 pips beyond the Asia range; beyond 50 it may be a real move, so skip (`Teoria 1 @ 27:46`; `Teoria 2 @ 23:03`; `Practica 1 @ 03:43`; `sin titulo @ 40:48`). An Asia range of about 27 pips was too small to trade (`Practica 5 @ 06:48`).
- Weekly structure: starts Sunday, up early, 2-3 days down, ends near where it began (`Teoria 4 @ 01:41`, `@ 03:33`). Mid-week reversal (`Teoria 1 @ 23:32`; `Niveles MFXM @ 21:06`). Rarely more than three days one way, and a fourth day reverses harder (`Teoria 1 @ 25:02`).
- Don't hold over the weekend (p.5).

## Stop and target placement

- **Stop, second-leg entry**: 7 pips plus spread beyond the high or low of day, so about 8-12 pips (`Teoria 2 @ 13:55`; `Practica 1 @ 06:47`; `Practica 7 @ 13:44`; `Practica 8 @ 06:38`). Seven is a minimum (`Practica 1 @ 34:23`). Use the first high of day, not the second (`Practica 6 @ 09:05`).
- **Stop, first-leg entry**: 20-25 pips (`Teoria 3 @ 21:17`; `Practica 2 @ 07:54`). TDI entries up to 25 (`Teoria 4 @ 25:53`).
- Older videos: about 15 pips past the day extreme (p.18); 20-30 pips past support or resistance (`Exponential Moving Average @ 17:15`; `Price Action 2 @ 08:05`); 20-25 pip stops at obvious spots get hunted (`sin titulo @ 35:26`).
- **Targets**: 30-50 pips per trade (`Teoria 2 @ 27:17`; `Practica 1 @ 18:17`; `Practica 5 @ 11:22`). Usual order: TP1 at EMA 50, the near edge of the Asia range or yesterday's high/low; TP2 at EMA 200 or the far edge of the Asia range; TP3 at the other day extreme (`Practica 1 @ 23:55`, `@ 28:24`; `Practica 2 @ 03:19`; `Practica 8 @ 03:49`). Other spots: ADR line, pivots (`Practica 6 @ 10:39`; `Practica 4 @ 15:20`).
- **Reward to risk** in the worked trades: mostly 1.5:1 to 3:1, e.g. 14 pips risked for 50 (`Practica 1 @ 18:40`).
- **Management**: partials at TP1 (`Teoria 5 @ 05:36`; `Practica 8 @ 03:55`). Break-even at about +30 pips (`Practica 1 @ 55:56`; `Teoria 3 @ 23:25`), though one trade moved it at +21 and another at +14 (`Practica 4 @ 03:19`; `Practica 8 @ 06:17`). Trail only past 30 pips (`Teoria 3 @ 23:14`). Exit on large pins against you (`Practica 2 @ 11:31`).
- **Two-hour rule**: if a trade is not working within 2 hours, close it at a small win or loss; the clock restarts when price retests the extreme (`Teoria 4 @ 33:23`, `@ 34:47`; `Teoria 2 @ 36:01`; `sin titulo @ 15:53`).
- After a stop-out, don't force it; wait for the next level, session or day (`Teoria 3 @ 20:01`).

## Risk per trade

- The only percent given: **2% of equity per trade** (`Practica 1 @ 18:45`). Trade Hub's built-in rules use 0.5-1% instead, a choice John can change per rule.
- Elsewhere the speakers talk lots, not percent: "good leverage, a big lot", bigger lots when the stop is short or all confirmations line up (`Teoria 2 @ 15:15`; `Teoria 3 @ 22:02`; `Practica 8 @ 11:06`). We do not copy this.
- Avoid several trades on the same currency at once (`Practica 8 @ 07:53`). Watch margin (`Practica 4 @ 05:16`).
- Claimed stats, untested: 90 of 100 trades win 30-50 pips, 10 lose 10-20 (`Teoria 2 @ 17:24`); rarely three losses in a row (`Teoria 3 @ 22:40`).
- Trade rarely; skip days with no perfect setup; don't chase a missed entry (`Teoria 2 @ 15:22`; `Practica 2 @ 05:58`).

## Other tools (TDI, ADR, pivots)

- **TDI** (RSI line inside Bollinger-style bands, with a red signal line): no settings are ever given (`Teoria 4 @ 22:05`; `Practica 6 @ 01:09`). Signals: the "shark fin" (aleta de tiburón), where the RSI line leaves the bands and comes back, then crosses the red line (`Teoria 4 @ 24:02`; `Practica 5 @ 11:30`; `Practica 1 @ 16:18`); divergence with price (`Practica 1 @ 12:47`); don't buy while the TDI is diving (`Practica 6 @ 09:51`). Close everything when the RSI re-enters the bands after a run (`Teoria 4 @ 27:45`).
- **ADR** (average daily range): about 200 pips a day and 600 a week at most (`Teoria 1 @ 13:16`, `@ 14:38`; p.14). Once a pair has used its usual range, a reversal is likely (`Practica 1 @ 03:13`; `Practica 3 @ 09:43`; `Practica 7 @ 16:20`). Price may test the ADR line several times before turning (`Teoria 1 @ 15:22`).
- **Pivots** (M1-M4, from yesterday's candle): possible high of day above, low below; buy between M1 and M2, sell between M3 and M4 (`Teoria 4 @ 14:55`; `Practica 4 @ 13:21`).
- **The 8-point checklist** kept beside every trade: TDI, shark fin, stop hunt, market-maker candles, divergence, pivots, ADR, yesterday's high/low (`Practica 8 @ 10:41`).
- **Zones** to watch: ADR high/low, stop-hunt zone, high/low of the day, yesterday's high/low (`Practica 1 @ 03:07`, `@ 61:46`).

Choices we made that the course does not state:

- RSI 40/60 instead of the course's 20-30 / 70-80, because our check runs on the confirming candle, after the RSI has turned.
- 120 hourly candles as "the week" and 24 as "the day": rolling windows, not 5 PM New York boundaries.
- Fixed R targets stand in for "first EMA, second EMA" targets. 3R on the second-leg rule mirrors 10 pips risked for 30-50.
- Percent buffers stand in for pips (0.1% is about 10 pips on EUR/USD).
- EMA 5 over 13 stands in for the fresh 5/13 cross, and EMA 12 over 36 for the 12/36 cross. We have no cross condition.
- The second-leg rule uses `nearLevel` instead of `formation`, because our `formation` check needs the second extreme to go **past** the first (the older videos' stop hunt), while the teoria/practica videos want the second leg to **hold** the day extreme.
- Our pattern detector tags plain Doji and Spinning Top as neutral, so a `pattern` condition can never match them. The rules list only directional candles (hammers, stars, engulfing, tweezers, dragonfly/gravestone doji).

## What Trade Hub can and cannot test

- **Forex is daily only** on the free feed (ECB rates, estimated highs and lows). Every 1h or 4h rule falls back to daily candles for forex, where session windows do nothing. So the intraday course setups (all built for 15m forex) only run on crypto.
- **Crypto 1h candles are flat.** They are built from CoinGecko's hourly price points, one point per hour, so open = high = low = close (2,159 of 2,160 bitcoin candles checked on 2026-11-10). This has two effects:
  - Wicks and candle shapes don't exist on 1h. The detector tags every flat candle as both Dragonfly Doji and Gravestone Doji, so any 1h rule listing those passes its `pattern` check on every candle. Today the candle confirmation in all four 1h Marvel rules is effectively switched off; their 1h results measure only the other conditions.
  - Swing stops on 1h sit at hourly closes, not real lows and highs.
    4h candles (4 points each) and 1d candles have some shape, but wicks are still understated.
- **No 15m or tick data**, so no 15m entry timing, no 7-pip stops measured from real wicks, and no spread model beyond the fee.
- **No news calendar**: the UK 4:30 AM news reversal, FOMC and NFP behavior cannot be tested.
- **Crypto trades 24/7** with no Asia box or London open in the forex sense, and no 5 PM day start, so the session ideas fit it only loosely.
- **Pips** become percents, so results don't map one-to-one to the course's pip numbers.

## Conditions to add (needs new code or better data)

Data first:

- **Real 1h OHLC for crypto** (for example CoinGecko `/ohlc` or an exchange API). A quicker fix is for the pattern detector to skip zero-range candles. Until one of those lands, 1h pattern checks mean nothing.
- **Intraday forex** (15m or 1h), needed for almost every course setup.

Conditions:

- **Asian range box**: Asia high and low (5 PM to about 3 AM New York), its size (skip under about 30 or over about 100 pips), and "price swept 25-50 pips past it but not more than 50".
- **Day / week reference levels** on 5 PM New York boundaries: high and low of day, yesterday's high and low, week's high and low, and "broke it but closed back inside". `nearLevel` only approximates these with a rolling window.
- **Second leg that holds the extreme**: an M/W whose second top or bottom stays inside the first (the opposite of today's `formation`), formed within 30-120 minutes.
- **ADR**: average daily range, "today's range already used up", and price at the ADR high or low.
- **Pivots M1-M4** from yesterday's candle.
- **Level counting**: three pushes with pauses labelled level 1/2/3, plus "no trades back toward a peak formation for 3 days" and "skip the fourth push".
- **EMA cross and EMA gap**: a fresh 5/13 or 12/36 cross, and "EMAs close together" versus "far apart" (level 3 hint).
- **Railroad tracks** candle pattern (two adjacent opposite candles of similar size). Not one of our 21 detectors.
- **Three vector candles**: three fast, similar-size candles one way out of the Asia range.
- **TDI**: RSI with Bollinger bands on the RSI and a signal line; "left the band, came back, crossed the signal line" (shark fin). The course never gives settings; common defaults are RSI 13, bands 34 with 1.6 deviation, signal 2 and 7 (not from the course).
- **Divergence**: price makes a new extreme while the RSI/TDI does not.
- **Weekday filter**: Tuesday to Thursday only, no new trades late Friday.
- **News calendar**: event time and currency, to test the post-news reversal and to stand aside around FOMC/NFP.
- **Exits**: two-hour time stop (reset on a retest of the extreme), partials at TP1 with break-even at about +30 pips, EMA-based targets (50, then 200), the opposite edge of the Asia range as a target, a trailing stop under EMA 12/36 or 5/13, and flat before the weekend.

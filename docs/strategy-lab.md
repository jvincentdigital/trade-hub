# Strategy lab

Added 2026-11-11. John (asleep, gave the go-ahead): "try a lot of strategies
and see which one is more effective... throw a lot of bots... combining them
all... my Fibonacci... a long trade, a day trade, a swing trade or a scalp
trade... backtest, look at the market a long time ago... use the Forex news
(red = could impact the market)."

The lab runs about 900 play-money bots over up to two years of history,
chooses each bot's few settings on the first 70% and scores it on the last
30% (walk-forward), runs the 1-hour-and-up bots once more on 2019-2024 history
nothing was chosen on (the holdout), and tests each bot against random sides
at the same moments. It is offline: it reads local history files, never the
network, a broker, `race.json` or the live race.

```bash
make lab                                   # all bots + report, about 30 seconds on the Mac Mini
make lab OUT=~/jvd-outputs/2026-11-11/strategy-lab
make lab-report OUT=...                    # rebuild REPORT.md / REPORT.html only
npx tsx scripts/lab/run.ts --only "fib-candle|15m"   # a subset, by bot id text
```

Output (default `~/jvd-outputs/<today>/strategy-lab`): `results.json` (every
bot, the top 5 and top 10 with every trade), `ranking.csv`, `ranking.md`,
`REPORT.md`, `REPORT.html` (phone page). Hand-written files in that folder go
into the report: `notes.md` as "What it means", `fixes.md` as the review
findings; a `run1-results.json` adds a before-and-after table.

Second run (2026-11-11 morning) after four reviewers' findings. What changed
and why is in "Review fixes" below; the first run's numbers were too kind.

## A bot

strategy x version x candle size x style x markets x news filter, one
$10,000 account. Sizing is the race's (`src/lib/race/account.ts`): 2% of the
balance per trade, at most 5 open, 10% open risk, position value at most free
balance x leverage (forex 30, gold and US30 20, Bitcoin 1). The trades of
every market merge into the account through the race's own
`portfolioBacktest`.

Markets: "all" (every market but Bitcoin, in one account) and each market
alone. Bitcoin is never pooled: at 1:1 its full-value margin blocked the
other markets and its trades were sized far under 2%.

Styles (London and New York local time, daylight saving included):

- scalp: entries until 16:00 New York, out by the end of its session (Asia
  ends 08:00 London, London ends 09:00 New York, New York ends 17:00 New York);
- day: entries until 16:00 New York, flat by 17:00 New York;
- swing: held at most 5 days; position: at most 30 days (the swing Fib on 4h
  and daily);
- race (built-in rules): stop or target only, at most 10 days.

News filter: off; block (no new trade from 30 min before to 30 min after a
red event in the market's currencies, open trades kept; BoJ days: 02:00-04:30
and 06:00-07:00 UTC); close (the same, and open trades close at the open of
the candle where the window starts); day (scalper only: no new trades on a
red-news day). Gold, US30 and Bitcoin follow USD news.

## Strategies and their free settings

Kept to 6 or fewer combinations each, so the walk-forward has little to fit.

**John's Fib** (`fib.ts`). Levels from his TradingView settings (0, 0.5,
0.618, 0.786, 0.886, 1, 2, -0.272, -0.414, -0.702). SELL: candle j sets the
lowest low of the last 12; walking back, "1" is the high of the most recent
bullish candle that no later bullish candle tops and that is at least k x
ATR(14) above the low (ATR read before it); "0" is the low. While armed, a
new low moves 0 (no entry on that candle), a trade past 1 cancels the setup,
and it expires after 24 candles. Entry: a limit at the 0.786 or 0.886 level
(a buy fills when the ask reaches it). Stop 1 + 0.25 ATR. Half off at 0, the
rest at -0.272. BUY mirrors it. Settings: k = 2, 3, 4; entry = 0.786, 0.886.
Versions: plain; + candle (a reversal candle that reaches the level, enter
at the next open); London session only (08:00-16:00 London time); + candle +
the day's London direction; + the bigger chart's trend (EMA 50 on 4h, or
daily for 4h bots); + candle + trend; and the **swing version** (added in the
second run): the move may take 40 candles, the setup lives 60, "1" is the
swing extreme of those 40 candles, k = 3, 4.5, 6, on 1h (swing style), 4h and
daily (position style).

John's two USD/JPY daily examples on the real candles (tests in
`fib.test.ts`, skipped where the history folder is missing): the 12-candle
rule finds his buy (1 = 153.240, 0 = 159.034 against his 153.174 and
159.032). His sell (1 = 163.738, 0 = 152.846) took 28 days; the 12-candle
rule anchors on the 09-01 bounce instead (160.273), the swing version finds
0 = 152.889 but puts 1 at the 07-23 high (163.989). His 163.738 is the
07-30 high, the first big candle of the drop. Open question for John: is "1"
the swing high, the last bullish candle before the drop, or the first candle
of the drop?

**Consolidation scalper** (`scalper.ts`), 5m and 15m. Range: the 20 candles
before. Squeeze: the Bollinger width (20, 2) of the candle before is in the
lowest 20% or 35% of its last 200. A reversal candle that reaches the edge's
15% zone and closes back inside (not past the middle) is faded at the next
open; stop 0.25 ATR past the edge or wick; target the middle or 90% across;
skipped under 1:1. A close more than 0.5 ATR outside the range stops fades
for 20 candles. Settings: target middle/far, squeeze 0.2/0.35.

**London breakout** (`london.ts`): the race's `londonBreakout`, unchanged,
called at every 15-minute tick from 06:15 to 10:00 UTC with the price the
tick would fill at (the open of the candle starting then), so its "no chase"
and "back inside the range" checks run as live, and a break stays usable for
2 closed candles; the race's margin floor on the stop; day style. Fade: the
opposite trade, same stop distance. Settings: target 1.5, 2, 3 x (fade 1,
1.5, 2). GBP/USD and gold. Fixed UTC hours on purpose (the live rule); the
report splits it by UK summer and winter time instead.

**The 9 built-in race rules** (`rules.ts`) on their own candle size with the
race's signals; setting: target 1.5, 2, 3 x the stop.

**Coin flip** (`control.ts`): about 1 entry in 20 candles, random side, stop
1.5 ATR, target 2x, 6 seeds, the luckiest kept in-sample. Shown for scale
only; it is no longer a pass mark.

**Combined accounts** (`runEnsemble`): several bots in one account, each
with the settings it chose alone in-sample.

## Engine (`engine.ts`)

The race's backtest rules: a signal enters at the next candle's open; when a
candle touches both stop and target, the stop counts; a gap through a level
fills at the open. Added:

- **Spread**: prices are BID, so a buy pays bid + spread and sells at the
  bid; a short sells at the bid and buys back at the ask, so its stop and
  target are checked on the ask.
- **Slippage** on every market fill (entry at the open, stop, time and news
  exits); none on limit fills (Fib entries, targets).
- **Cost by time** (`costMultAt`, forex, gold, US30; Bitcoin none): spread x4
  and slippage x2 on 21:00-23:00 UTC (rollover); spread x8 and slippage x5 on
  the first candle after a gap (Sunday reopen, the daily gold and US30 halt);
  spread x3 and slippage x5 on a candle with red news in it (5 min before to
  15 after; BoJ its whole windows); x1.5 at the Asian open (23:00-01:00 UTC,
  forex and gold); x2.5 for US30 outside New York cash hours. A fill at a
  candle's open uses what holds at that instant; stops and targets inside a
  candle up to 1h use the candle's span; longer candles only their open.
- **Swap** (`swapCost`): a yearly rate on the position value, long and short,
  per weekday rollover held (22:00 UTC), Wednesday counting 3 for forex and
  gold, Friday for US30; a side that would earn carry is charged 0. Folded
  into the exit price, so dollars and R include it.
- A limit fill never reaches its target inside its own candle (it may be
  stopped there).
- Half off at a first target; the trade's exit is the blended price.
- Options: `independent` (every entry on its own, for per-signal stats),
  `flatCosts` (the first run's costs), `limitFromNext` (see the luck test).
- Each trade records its swap nights and whether it was held over a weekend.
- P&L converts at the exit rate (`usdPerQuoteExit` in the race's
  `Candidate`): USD/JPY at 1/exit, EUR/GBP at the last GBP/USD 1h candle
  closed by then.

| Market  | Spread  | Slippage | Commission / side | Swap long / short a year |
| ------- | ------- | -------- | ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| GBP/USD | 1.0 pip | 0.2 pip  | 0.0025%           | 1% / 1%                  |
| EUR/USD | 0.8 pip | 0.2 pip  | 0.0025%           | 2.5% / 0.5%              |
| EUR/GBP | 1.2 pip | 0.2 pip  | 0.0025%           | 2.5% / 0.5%              |
| USD/JPY | 1.0 pip | 0.2 pip  | 0.0025%           | 0.5% / 4.5%              |
| Gold    | $0.30   | $0.05    | 0.001%            | 5% / 1%                  |
| US30    | 2 pts   | 0.5 pt   | none              | 6% / 1%                  |
| Bitcoin | 0.02%   | 0.01%    | 0.1%              | none (exchange spot)     |

## Scoring (`walkforward.ts`, `bots.ts`, `run.ts`)

- **Walk-forward.** Trades split by entry time: first 70% in-sample, last 30%
  out-of-sample, each in its own fresh $10,000 account. The settings with the
  most in-sample money among those with at least 20 in-sample trades are
  kept. Out-of-sample results never feed back.
- **Each signal on its own.** Every planned trade also runs alone: average R
  after costs, standard error and t, the same signals with zero and double
  costs (a fixed set, so trade order cannot bend the cost sweep), longs and
  shorts apart.
- **Random-direction (luck) test.** Every signal is also traded the other
  way at the same moment, stop and target mirrored around the entry price.
  With no skill in the side, real and mirrored would win equally often; z =
  sum(d) / sqrt(sum(d^2)) with d = (real R - mirrored R) / 2 (the exact
  sign-flip test, normal approximation), one-sided p. For limit entries both
  real and mirrored check exits only from the next candle (`limitFromNext`):
  the worst-case "stop first" inside the fill candle hits the mirrored trade
  far more often, because price came from its stop side, which made 110 of
  233 Fib bots look skilled before this fix. Checked on the coin flips: 1 of
  40 under 0.05 out-of-sample. Holm over all distinct bots with 30+
  out-of-sample trades.
- **Copies.** Bots of the same strategy, chart and markets with exactly the
  same out-of-sample trades (most "off" and "block" pairs) count once.
- **Fragility.** Out-of-sample net without its 3 best trades, a bootstrap
  95% range of the average trade, weekend-held trades' net, UK summer and
  winter, and a neighbour grid in the details (all settings; for race rules
  also stop buffer +-0.1/0.2%, RSI levels +-5, without the candle condition).
- **Holdout.** 1h, 4h and daily bots: once on 2019 to 2024-11 with the
  settings chosen in-sample. Nothing reads it to choose anything.
- **Ranking** (`ranking.csv`): the lower of the in-sample and out-of-sample
  t of the per-signal average R, so both halves must hold. In-sample losers
  that won out-of-sample get their own "probably luck" table.
- Drawdown is on the closed-trade balance (open trades not marked to market).

## Top 5 in the live race

Rule (fixed before looking at the second run's list): at least 30
out-of-sample trades, out-of-sample drawdown under 25%, in-sample profit
factor at least 1 and out-of-sample at most 0.5 above it, ranked by
out-of-sample profit factor, copies merged, at most 2 per strategy family
(all Fib versions are one family, both London versions one, each rule its
own), the best qualifying Fib takes 5th place if none made it. Added after
the first look: news versions of the same strategy, chart and market count
once (two near-copies would waste a race slot). The holdout is not read.

They join the live race as their own accounts (`src/lib/race/labBots.ts`,
`LAB_BOTS`) through `syncRaceState`, at the race's start money ($10,000 since
2026-11-11), without touching any other account or open trade. They run on
every tick (the 15-minute London ticks and the hourly one).

How the live race runs them (`labCheck`, `stepStrategy`):

- **Rules**: the race's `latestSignal` with the bot's own target (rr) on
  closed candles, opened at the latest price like every race trade.
- **John's Fib**: `johnsFib` on closed candles; a level touched in one of the
  last 2 closed candles opens at the price now, unless that price has already
  gone more than 25% of the way from the level to 0 ("no chase"). The lab
  fills a limit at the level, so live fills are a little different.
- **First target**: `target1` and `part1` on the live trade; half closes there
  (a closed trade with `part1Of`), the rest runs to the target or stop.
- **No deadline live since 2026-11-12**: live trades hold until their stop
  or target (`docs/strategy-race.md`, "2026-11-12: trades hold"). Until then
  a `deadline` on the live trade (race style 10 days, swing 5, day 17:00 New
  York) closed it. The lab's backtests keep their time exits, so live
  results may differ from them.
- **News**: Forex Factory's free weekly JSON (`newsFeed.ts`, no key), High
  impact only, cached as `ff-thisweek.json` next to the race file and
  downloaded at most every 6 hours (a failed download uses the cached copy,
  or turns the filter off for that tick and logs it). "block" bots open
  nothing inside a window; "close" bots also open nothing when a window
  starts within the next candle, and close their trades then (reason
  `news`).
- **US30** is a race-only market (`RACE_ONLY_MARKETS`: leverage 20, fee
  0.0025% a side, about the lab's spread), not in the app's instrument list
  (the other pages have no US30 feed). Prices: Dow futures `YM=F` from Yahoo
  Finance's public chart feed (no key, unofficial, delayed, play money only;
  trades about 23 hours a day like the lab's US30; the cash index `^DJI`
  only has 09:30-16:00 New York). Futures sit a little above the cash index.
- **Gold 4h** uses Kraken's own 4h candles (UTC), not New York-close ones:
  the EMA 200 needs more 4h candles than Kraken's 30 days of 1h can build.
  On UTC 4h candles the same bot made +$166 out-of-sample (in-sample
  +$9,026) instead of +$1,953.
- No scalp bot made the top 5, so no 5-minute live feed was added (Kraken
  has `interval=5` if one is needed later).
- New exit reasons `time` and `news` in `closedLiveTradeSchema`: deploy the
  site (it parses the race state) before the Mac Mini runs the new tick.

## John's London v1 (`johnsLondon.ts`, added 2026-11-11)

A separate, frozen strategy, not one of the bots above. The rules live in
`~/jvd-outputs/2026-11-11/london-strategy/SPEC.md`; the code follows it
section by section (comments point to the spec numbers). Rewritten the same
afternoon after John's answers (`JOHN-ANSWERS.md`): the old stop-and-reverse
flip is gone, and v1-flip, v1-NY and thirds are new.

- **v1**: GBP/USD and gold only, 15-minute candles built from the 1-minute
  BID files. Entries from 07:00 to 15:00 London, flat by 16:30 London (UK
  summer time aware). After a big move (k ATRs inside 12 candles) the Fib has
  1 at the last opposite candle and 0 at the end of the move. A closed candle
  that reaches 0.786 without passing 1 is the signal; the trade opens at the
  next 1-minute open. Stop past 1 by b ATRs, at least 10 round trips of costs,
  plan between 1.5R and 2.5R. Half off at 0, the rest at -0.272. No trades 30
  minutes around red news, at most 2 trades per market per day.
- **One dollar bet (2.8)**: GBP/USD and gold never sit on opposite dollar
  sides in one account (GBP/USD long and gold long are "USD down"). A trade
  that reaches its stop or target later inside the minute of the new fill
  still counts as open; one force-closed at that minute's open does not.
- **Race hours** (John's live rules, a backstop in the code): entries only
  02:00 to 12:30 AST (06:00 to 16:30 UTC), flat at 12:45 AST (live trades
  hold since 2026-11-12; the backtest keeps this). Every v1 and
  v1-NY moment is inside them, so they never change a backtest trade (a test
  walks a whole year of sessions to show it).
- **v1-flip (section 3)**: v1 plus "the ride": on a setup's first move candle
  (the arming candle, or a candle that moved 0) that closes with the move and
  passes the size test (v1's plan at a fill exactly at 0.786 pays at least g,
  before the 2.5R cap), a market order with the move. Stop at that 0.786 level
  (fixed, cost floor), one target at 0 - ext x (1 - 0), 120 minutes at most,
  and closed at the open of the minute in which its own setup's Fib entry
  fills. Only a market's first trade of the day; then v1's Fib entry as usual.
  `trackDay` reports each setup's move candles and its Fib signal;
  `rideSignals` and `prepareRide` are pure.
- **v1-NY (section 3A)**: its own account. New York clock (`nySessionFor`, US
  summer time): range 08:00 to 08:30 NY from 1-minute candles, London's high
  and low from 07:00 London to 08:00 NY. The first c-minute candle (15 or 5)
  that closes past the break line (w = on: also past London's high or low) is
  the break: stop past the far side of the range by b ATRs, one target at
  1.5R, fill at most 0.1 x the stop worse than the close. Then v1's Fib entry
  on the break's side after the break candle (trackers start empty at 08:00
  NY). Also reports London's lines (break or rejection at London's high and
  low, 08:00 to 11:00 NY).
- **Thirds (section 3B)**: a Fib entry whose signal candle already closed our
  way goes in at once. Otherwise a ladder: thirds at 0.786, 0.836 and 0.886
  (each at a candle close, a third of 2%), one stop from v1's rule at the last
  rung, and one add at the first turn candle that brings the trade to 2% and
  moves the stop behind the pullback (never further). `walkParts` walks the
  whole trade; the account takes parts (`addPart`) and re-walked fills
  (`replaceFills`). R = net dollars / 2% of the balance at the first part.
- **Code**: `src/lib/lab/johnsLondon.ts` (pure: signals, plans, 1-minute exit
  walks, one account per run, mirror, coin flips, pick rules, the loader
  guard) and `johnsLondon.test.ts` (one test per rule, look-ahead guards for
  the ride, the New York range and the thirds' turn candle, the caps, no
  loops, and the spec's check values 2.9, M6, N9 on the design data when the
  files are there). It reuses `impulseAt` and `fibLevel` (`fib.ts`),
